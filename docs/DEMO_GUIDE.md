# VistaVision — 5-Minute School Demo Guide

**Before students arrive (10 min):** start the app on HTTPS (or localhost on the demo laptop); open *Settings* and confirm the AI server is connected; allow the camera once; have a white sheet and a thick dark pen, plus an apple/banana/cup; press *Reset session* so charts start clean.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Home page → **Let's Start** | "AI learns patterns from examples. Today you teach it with your own faces, writing and objects." |
| 0:30–1:45 | **Smart Recognition → Face Expressions → Start Camera.** Invite 2–3 students to pull faces. | "It finds each face and *estimates* a visible expression. It can be wrong, and it never knows who you are. The numbers are temporary IDs." Point at *Faces now* vs *Tracked* and the *Uncertain* label. |
| 1:45–2:45 | **Handwritten Numbers → Draw on screen.** A student draws 7, then 43 in *Several digits*. | "The model learned from tens of thousands of handwritten digits. We tidy your drawing to look like those." Show confidence and the other guesses. **Press ✅ or ❌ under the result** to tell the AI whether it was right. |
| 2:45–3:15 | **Write on paper** → camera → Capture → Recognize. | "Same model, but now it has to cope with a camera, shadows and your handwriting." |
| 3:15–3:45 | **Object Explorer** → show an apple → Analyze. Then show something unusual. | "It knows 80 kinds of things. When it's unsure or the object isn't one of them, it can be wrong." |
| 3:45–4:30 | **Data Visualization** → show charts, filter by origin (AI vs manual). Add a manual record live. | "Every guess we just made became data. Data scientists turn data into charts to find patterns." |
| 4:30–4:45 | Open the **Human vs AI scoreboard** on the dashboard. | "You just graded the AI. Where did it do well? Where did it get confused?" |
| 4:45–5:00 | Press **Stop Camera**; show *Session Report*. | "Nothing was saved: no photos, no names. Responsible AI means being honest about limits." |

**Discussion questions:** Why did it say Uncertain? What would make a digit harder to read? Is a "Happy" label the same as being happy? What data would you collect to improve it?

**If something goes wrong:** camera blocked → use Draw, Upload or *Load Sample Dataset*; server offline → manual charts still work; misreads are a teaching moment about model limits.
